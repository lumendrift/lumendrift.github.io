AI commerce SEO

How AI agents rank stores and what to optimize beyond classical SEO.

How agents rank stores

Classical SEO optimizes for crawlers. AI commerce SEO optimizes for agents. Agents rank by: protocol completeness, response latency, schema fidelity, and price/availability accuracy.

What to optimize

  • Manifest schema completeness.
  • Endpoint latency under 250ms p95.
  • Inventory accuracy versus the displayed catalog.
  • Stable, idempotent checkout under retries.

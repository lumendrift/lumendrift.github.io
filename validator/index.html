Validator

Score your storefront against the UCP spec

Free, read-only, public-endpoint validation in under 30 seconds.

What we check

Six categories, one weighted score

Manifest schema

Discoverability, version, fields, signatures.

Endpoint reachability

HTTP semantics, content types, error shape.

Authentication & identity

Supported agent identity schemes.

Catalog accessibility

Required fields, currency, pagination, locale.

Checkout flow

Idempotency, totals, taxes, error states.

Protocol compliance

CORS, rate limits, retry semantics.

Why it matters

If agents can't transact, you lose the customer

Autonomous shopping agents are routing traffic and revenue. Stores that fail validation simply aren't in the consideration set.
